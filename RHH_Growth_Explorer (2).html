Plan comparisons use the investor plan: 25% quarterly growth at full sales pace and utilization. Each state caps at 75 rural and 6 regional clinics; overflow opens new states at Alaska pricing. Rural clinics start at 4 hours a month and mature at 16 hours a week (75% Specialty Access, 25% Medical Staff Expansion); regional hospitals grow from 4 to 40 Specialty Access hours a week. RHH pays RTS $225 per Specialty Access hour and $275 per Medical Staff Expansion hour in every state. The team grows with the business: sales with signings; account managers, credentialing, recruiting and engineering with live clinics; a CTO, Chief Medical Officer and VP Sales at 150, 200 and 300 live clinics; and executive pay steps up 15% at $25M, $50M and $100M revenue run-rate. Sales commissions: subscription fees only: Texas 10% of first-year subscription; other states 20% of first-year subscription, then 10% of subscription fees each year after for customer service. Regional subscriptions are $15K a month in every state. Legacy management fees are held flat. Raise rule: the deepest new-model cash dip plus six months of 2027 new fixed costs, rounded up to $250K. Projections are estimates for discussion, not a forecast of results.
